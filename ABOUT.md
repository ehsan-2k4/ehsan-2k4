## How I Work

1. **Specify** — the problem, the constraints, and what's explicitly out of scope.
2. **Review** — attack my own spec before reality does.
3. **Build** — implementation is mechanical when the spec is honest.
4. **Verify** — behavior matches the document, or one of them is wrong.
5. **Version** — every change gets a changelog entry.

---

## Principles I Actually Use

- Ambiguity is a bug — resolve it in the doc, not in the diff.
- Constraints are features. An unbounded scope never ships.
- Predictable > clever. Future-me is a different developer.
- Design is engineering with a palette.
- Every system deserves one small piece of delight.
